Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · Mune Technologies Inc.

The short version: Your financial data is yours. We never sell it, share it with employers or third parties without your consent, or use it to target you with ads. Bank statements are deleted the moment we finish reading them. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why, and how you can remove it.

Who we are

Mune Technologies Inc. ("munē," "we," "us") is a Delaware C-Corporation that operates the munē financial wellness platform at getmune.com and app.getmune.com. This Privacy Policy applies to all munē products and services.

What we collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Your name and email address when you create an account
  • Your quiz responses — behavioral questions only, no financial data at this stage
  • Bank statements you upload for Moment analysis
  • Information you share with munē Chat
  • Blueprint allocations and goal information you enter

Information we derive from your data:

  • Your Munē Type — derived from quiz responses and behavioral patterns
  • Financial pattern analysis — derived from statement uploads
  • Blueprint recommendations — derived from your income and allocation inputs

Information collected automatically:

  • Usage data — which features you use and how often
  • Device and browser information for security and compatibility
  • Authentication tokens to keep you signed in securely

Bank statements

We delete your bank statement the moment we finish reading it. munē does not store raw financial documents. We extract patterns and figures from your statement, then immediately delete the original file. We do not access your bank accounts directly unless you explicitly connect them.

How we use your information

  • To provide and personalize your munē experience
  • To generate your Munē Type, Blueprint, and behavioral analysis
  • To power munē Chat with context about your financial situation
  • To send transactional emails — account verification, receipts, important updates
  • To improve the munē system through aggregate, anonymized usage patterns

We do not use your data to target you with advertising. munē does not sell your data. munē does not share your individual financial data with employers, even if your employer sponsors your access.

Data sharing

We share data only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: Supabase (database and authentication), Resend (transactional email), Stripe (payment processing), Anthropic (AI processing). Each is bound by data processing agreements.
  • Legal requirements: If required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of munē users.
  • Business transfers: If munē is acquired, your data will transfer to the acquiring entity under equivalent privacy protections.

We do not share your individual financial data, Munē Type, Blueprint, conversation history, or personal profile with any employer, organization, or third party for any commercial purpose.

Organizational accounts

If your munē access is sponsored by an employer, ERG, or organization, they receive only aggregate, anonymized program data — engagement rates, wellness trends, and educational interest patterns across the participant group. They never see your individual data, financial situation, Munē Type, Blueprint allocations, munē Chat conversations, or personal goals.

Your rights

  • Access: You can request a copy of your data at any time
  • Correction: You can update your information in your account settings
  • Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated data at any time
  • Portability: You can request your data in a portable format
  • Opt out: You can opt out of non-transactional communications at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@getmune.com.

Data security

We use industry-standard security practices including encryption in transit and at rest, row-level security on all database tables, and authentication through Supabase's secure auth system. We do not store API keys or sensitive credentials in client-side code.

Children's privacy

munē is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, contact us immediately at privacy@getmune.com.

Changes to this policy

We will notify you of material changes to this policy by email and by posting a notice in the app. Continued use of munē after the effective date of any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
