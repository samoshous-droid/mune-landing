Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Mune Technologies Inc.

The short version: Your financial data is yours. We do not sell your personal information or use your financial information for targeted advertising. We use the information you provide to operate Munē, respond to inquiries, and improve the service.

Who we are

Mune Technologies Inc. ("munē," "we," "us") operates getmune.com and the Munē application. This policy describes how we handle information across the website, application, and inquiry forms.

What we collect

Account and product information:

  • Name, email address, and account information you provide.
  • Quiz responses, Blueprint inputs, goals, and information you choose to share in Munē.
  • Financial documents or financial information you choose to provide for analysis.

Website inquiry information:

  • Name, email address, optional phone number, organization or community information, selected topic, and the message you submit through Work With Munē.
  • Basic technical information used to protect forms from spam and abuse.

Usage and technical information:

  • Device, browser, authentication, and service-usage information needed for security, reliability, and product improvement.

Financial documents

If you upload a financial document, we process it to provide the requested Munē experience. We limit access to the systems and service providers needed to process that information. We do not sell uploaded financial documents or use them for advertising. Retention and deletion practices depend on the specific product workflow and technical configuration; we do not promise immediate deletion unless that behavior has been verified for that workflow.

How we use information

  • Provide and personalize the Munē experience.
  • Generate financial behavior insights, Blueprint outputs, and relevant educational support.
  • Respond to Personal Support, Organization, and Community inquiries.
  • Send transactional or service-related communications.
  • Protect the service, prevent abuse, and improve reliability.

Service providers

We may use service providers to operate Munē, including hosting, database, authentication, payment, AI-processing, analytics, and email-delivery providers. Website inquiries may be processed through Vercel infrastructure and delivered through Resend. These providers process information on our behalf to provide their services.

Organizations and communities

We do not provide an employer, organization, or community sponsor with an individual's private financial details, account credentials, personal conversations, or personal Blueprint information merely because that organization sponsors or refers access. Any future organization reporting should be designed around aggregate or appropriately limited information and disclosed before use.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information we maintain about you, subject to legal and operational requirements. Contact privacy@getmune.com.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect information. No system can guarantee absolute security, so do not send passwords, Social Security numbers, full account numbers, or other highly sensitive credentials through website inquiry forms.

Children's privacy

Munē is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the service.

Changes

We may update this policy as Munē changes. Material updates will be posted here with a revised date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: privacy@getmune.com
