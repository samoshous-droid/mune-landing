Legal

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026 — pre-launch draft for legal review · Mune Technologies Inc.

The short version: Your financial data is yours. We never sell it, share it with employers or third parties without your consent, or use it to target you with ads. Original files are read in the browser; extracted financial text is sent to an AI processor. Provider retention and deletion must be verified before real-data release. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why, and how you can remove it.

Who we are

Mune Technologies Inc. ("munē," "we," "us") operates the munē financial wellness platform at getmune.com and mune-app.vercel.app. This Privacy Policy applies to all munē products and services.

What we collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Your name and email address when you create an account
  • Your quiz responses — behavioral questions only, no financial data at this stage
  • Bank statements you upload for Moment analysis
  • Information you share with munē Chat
  • Blueprint allocations and goal information you enter

Information we derive from your data:

  • Your Munē Type — derived from quiz responses and behavioral patterns
  • Financial pattern analysis — derived from statement uploads
  • Blueprint recommendations — derived from your income and allocation inputs

Information collected automatically:

  • Usage data — which features you use and how often
  • Device and browser information for security and compatibility
  • Authentication tokens to keep you signed in securely

Bank statements

Original files and extracted data are different. The browser reads the original file locally. Extracted financial text is sent through Munē to Anthropic for analysis. Derived summaries and insights are saved to your account. We do not claim immediate deletion across processor systems, logs or backups. Statement processing remains restricted until retention and cleanup controls are verified. Bank connections are not enabled.

How we use your information

  • To provide and personalize your munē experience
  • To generate your Munē Type, Blueprint, and behavioral analysis
  • To power munē Chat with context about your financial situation
  • To send transactional emails — account verification, receipts, important updates
  • To improve the munē system through aggregate, anonymized usage patterns

We do not use your data to target you with advertising. munē does not sell your data. munē does not share your individual financial data with employers, even if your employer sponsors your access.

Data sharing

We share data only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: Supabase (database and authentication), Resend (transactional email), Stripe (payment processing), Anthropic (AI processing). Provider contracts, processing settings and retention require verification before real-data release.
  • Legal requirements: If required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of munē users.
  • Business transfers: If munē is acquired, your data will transfer to the acquiring entity under equivalent privacy protections.

Sponsors do not purchase access to individual finances. Service providers process information to deliver the service, as described above; that processing is distinct from selling data or providing individual financial records to an employer.

Organizational accounts

If your munē access is sponsored by an employer, ERG, or organization, reporting is restricted until group-privacy controls are verified. Small groups, identifying breakdowns and repeated comparisons can reveal individuals, so reports may be withheld. They never see your individual data, financial situation, Munē Type, Blueprint allocations, munē Chat conversations, or personal goals.

Your rights

  • Access: You can request a copy of your data at any time
  • Correction: You can update your information in your account settings
  • Deletion: You can request deletion of your account and associated data; legally required records and backup retention may have exceptions. Contact privacy@getmune.com if automatic deletion is unavailable
  • Portability: You can request your data in a portable format
  • Opt out: You can opt out of non-transactional communications at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@getmune.com.

Data security

Munē uses Supabase for authentication and database services. Account isolation, authorization, retention, operator access and recovery controls require independent staging verification before real-data release. This notice is not a security certification.

Children's privacy

The initial Munē app is for adults aged 18 and older. Youth education workshops do not provide app eligibility. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us immediately at privacy@getmune.com.

Changes to this policy

We will notify you of material changes to this policy by email and by posting a notice in the app. Continued use of munē after the effective date of any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
