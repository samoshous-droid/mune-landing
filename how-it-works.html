How munē works

From where you are
to where you want to be.

munē does not tell you what everyone else does. It learns your actual financial reality — your patterns, your income, your goals — and builds a personalized system from there.

The munē system

Five steps. One continuous loop.

01
Understand
Connect your financial reality

Upload a bank statement or connect your accounts. munē reads your real financial picture — income, spending, patterns, obligations — and names what is actually happening. Your statement is deleted the moment we finish reading it.

02
Build
Your personalized Blueprint

munē creates your Blueprint — a personalized plan built from your real numbers. Five allocations: Obligations, Safety, Flexible, Debt Attack, and Guilt-Free. One behavioral rule for 30 days. Locked. Specific. Yours.

03
Act
One clear focus every day

Home shows you one thing to focus on today — drawn from your Blueprint rule, your pattern, and your behavioral history. Not a list of everything. The one decision that matters most right now, with the context to make it.

04
Adapt
The system moves with you

When income changes, when life shifts, when you drift — munē adjusts. Drift is detected at two missed days. Recovery is three-tiered and shame-free. The system never punishes you for being human. It routes you back.

05
Grow
Proof the system is working

Journey records every chapter — days followed, decisions made, goals set, milestones reached. The Receipt is permanent proof of what has changed. A year from now, you will be able to look back and see exactly how far you have come.
