Privacy — non-negotiable

Individual financial data
stays individual.

This is not a compliance statement. It is the reason people will actually use the program. When employees trust that their financial life is private, they engage honestly. That is what creates real change.

Organizations never see individual transactions, balances, or debts.
Blueprint allocations, munē Chat conversations, and personal goals are never shared with employers, managers, or HR.
Munē Types and behavioral patterns remain private. Aggregate pattern trends are available — individual classifications are not.
Participants own their data. When someone leaves the organization, their munē account remains entirely theirs.
Bank statements are deleted immediately after analysis. munē never stores raw financial documents.
