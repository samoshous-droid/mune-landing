About munē

Built by someone

Most financial tools treat money as a math problem. munē treats it as a human one. That difference starts with who built it and why.

Where this comes from

Most people were never taught how money actually works. Not in school. Not at home. The information existed — it just was not available to everyone. It moved through certain families, certain advisors, certain zip codes. Everyone else figured it out the hard way — or did not figure it out at all.

The communities that built this country — that did the work, raised the families, held the institutions together — are the same communities most financial tools were never designed for. Generic budgeting apps. Advice that assumes a financial advisor. Literacy programs that teach vocabulary without changing behavior.

munē exists because the problem is not knowledge. It is access. The system was always possible. It just was not built for everyone.

The founder

15 years in finance.
A lifetime of watching
what gets left out.

Sam Oshobukola has spent his career at the intersection of finance and the communities traditional finance ignores. Banking at Capital One. Tax preparation at H&R Block. Investing at Morgan Stanley. Stock exchanges at Nasdaq and NYSE. Insurance at Allianz. Corporate finance at Exelon.

Before building munē, he delivered financial literacy workshops directly to working communities — real people, real questions, real gaps in what they had been given access to. Those workshops are where munē started.

munē is the system he wished he could have handed every person who walked into those rooms.

Sam Oshobukola
Founder & CEO · Mune Technologies Inc.
