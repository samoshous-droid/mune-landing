Every workshop is taught directly by the founder of Mune Technologies — the same person who built the six-archetype framework, the Munē Score, and the app itself. This isn't outsourced to a training team or delivered from a script written by someone else.

The content was tested in person, with real people navigating real financial pressure, long before a single line of app code existed. The app is the workshop, systemized. The workshop is the app, taught live.

What it actually is

Not a benefits package.
A session, and a system to keep.

01

A single session, taught live

In person or virtual. Built on the same identity-first approach that runs the app — this isn't a generic budgeting seminar, it starts with who your people are with money before what they should do about it.

02

Individual munē access for every participant

Everyone leaves with their own account on the same app every munē user has — same free tier, same $9.99/mo Personal tier. Not a separate enterprise product. The same system, so the room doesn't end when the workshop does.

03

No dashboards, no individual data shared with your organization

What happens in someone's munē account is theirs. Your organization gets the workshop and the goodwill of having offered it — not a window into anyone's finances.

A small number, on purpose.

This is taught personally, which means it doesn't scale the way software does. Mune Technologies is currently taking on a limited number of organizations at a time. If the timing doesn't work out immediately, it's capacity, not disinterest.
