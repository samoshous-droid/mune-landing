# Munē public claims rules

For each change, define the customer need and acceptance criteria. Follow the release policy, gate evidence and claims register in the companion mune-app repository (docs/release-policy.md, docs/release-evidence.json, docs/claims-register.json).

- Verify every factual, pricing, product, privacy, founder, patent, scientific and outcome assertion before publication; record its evidence, limits, owner and affected pages in the claims register.
- Match copy to the actual candidate app behavior/configuration and authorized release scope. A feature advertised or implemented is not thereby verified.
- Do not imply credentials establish regulatory approval, institutional endorsement, scientific validity or financial accuracy.
- Do not claim immediate deletion, anonymity, unlimited service or measured impact without scoped supporting evidence.
- Keep draft legal notices until qualified counsel approves actual behavior and operating facts. Never edit away a launch blocker.
- Use synthetic information in examples. Do not expose customer finances or identifying information in analytics, logs, screenshots or review evidence.
- Handoffs must distinguish implemented, verified, unverified and release decision. Failed or missing material evidence blocks publication of the affected promise.
- Check critical mobile/accessibility journeys, pricing arithmetic, links and consistency with app limits. Document actual results and limitations.
- Follow the app release gate before coordinated publication. A draft PR is not production approval.
