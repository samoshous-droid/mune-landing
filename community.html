Why community matters

The knowledge that transforms financial lives has always existed.
It just was not available to everyone.

Traditional financial education reaches people who already have access — through employers, financial advisors, college courses, and family. The communities that need it most are the ones most often left out.

munē was built by someone from those communities, for those communities. The workshops, the programs, and the sponsored access model all exist because financial knowledge should not be a privilege. It should be a foundation.

What munē brings to your community

Programs built for real life — not a classroom setting.

01
munē Workshops
Single session · 60–90 minutes

A practical, judgment-free financial education session built around your community's reality. Covers behavioral patterns, the Blueprint system, and one clear action every participant can take the same day. Not a lecture. A conversation that leads somewhere.

02
Financial Wellness Series
Multi-session · 4–8 weeks

A structured program that takes participants from awareness to action over multiple sessions. Each session builds on the last — patterns, Blueprint, Moment, Vision, and ongoing accountability. For communities ready to go deeper than a one-time event.

03
munē Access
Continued learning · ongoing

After the workshop ends, learning should not. munē Access gives participants continued access to the full munē system — their Blueprint, Moment, munē Chat, and Journey — so the work they started does not stop when the session does.

04
Community Challenges
Structured initiative · 30–90 days

A community-wide financial behavior initiative. A shared goal, a shared timeline, and individual progress that rolls up to a collective outcome. Churches, schools, ERGs, and nonprofits have used challenges to create accountability that feels like belonging.

Who brings munē to their community

Wherever people gather, munē can meet them.

Any organization that cares about the financial wellbeing of the people it serves can bring munē to their community — in person, virtually, or both.

Faith Communities
Churches & houses of worship

Financial stewardship is a core value in most faith traditions. munē gives congregations a practical, judgment-free way to live it out — without shame, without complexity.

Education
Schools, colleges & universities

Students graduate with degrees and no financial foundation. munē workshops and series give them the system the curriculum never did — before the real financial decisions start.

Nonprofits
Community organizations & social services

For organizations serving communities navigating financial hardship — munē meets people where they are, without judgment, and gives them a real system to build from.

Youth Programs
Youth organizations & after-school programs

Financial habits form early. munē workshops adapted for youth give young people the foundation that changes the trajectory — before the patterns are set.

ERGs & Community Groups
Employee Resource Groups & community initiatives

ERGs that want to go beyond networking and create real impact bring munē as a community program — workshops, series, and sponsored access for members.

Sponsored access

Fund the access. They keep the system.

Organizations, foundations, and employers can sponsor munē access for a community — covering the cost so participants can continue using the full system after the workshop ends, at no cost to them.

You cover the cost

Sponsored participants have their own private munē account. Their data is theirs. When the sponsorship ends, they can continue on their own — or you renew it.
You see the impact
Aggregate program data shows engagement, wellness trends, and behavioral progress — so you can report on the impact of the investment to funders, boards, or leadership.
Built for grant reporting
If your organization funds community programs with grant money, munē's aggregate outcome data provides the evidence your funders want to see.
