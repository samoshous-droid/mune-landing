Why this matters

The knowledge gap is not a personal failure. It is a structural one.

Communities with generational wealth pass down financial knowledge the same way they pass down assets — through role models, conversations, and access to professional guidance.

Communities without that foundation pass down the survival strategies they had — which are often not the strategies that build.

munē was built to close that gap. Not with generic advice. With personalized direction built around who someone actually is and where they actually are.

Privacy commitment

Their financial life remains theirs.

Community partners never see individual financial data. The relationship between a person and their munē is private — always.

Partner organizations do not receive individual balances, transactions, debts, or spending patterns
Advisor conversations are never visible to any organization
Individual Blueprints and financial profiles are private to the user
Program reporting uses aggregate, anonymized data only — never individual data
Users can cancel, delete their data, or leave a program at any time

Before munē existed, the workshops gave people knowledge with no system to use it. Now the system exists. The loop is closed.
